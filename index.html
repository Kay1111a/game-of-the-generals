Rules & ranks

Each player secretly arranges 21 pieces in their nearest three rows. Leave six squares empty. On your turn, move one piece one square horizontally or vertically. Choose an opposing piece to challenge it.

Winning the match

Capture the enemy Flag, or take your Flag to the far row. If an enemy is next to your Flag when it arrives, it must survive their next move. An attacking Flag wins against a defending Flag and loses against any other rank.

Challenges

Higher officers beat lower officers and Privates. A Spy beats any officer. A Private beats a Spy. Equal ranks remove each other, except when two Flags meet. In standard play the arbiter announces the outcome and keeps enemy ranks hidden.

    Ending a match

    Offer a draw on your turn. Play continues if your opponent declines. Use Resign to concede or record a forfeit. Threefold repetition is an optional tournament convention in this edition.
