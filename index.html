    How to play

    Start off the board at 0. Roll one six-sided die, follow the numbers and climb a ladder when you land at its foot. Landing on a snake’s head sends you down to its tail. Players can share a square.

    Reach 100 exactly to win. An overshoot keeps you on your square. Each player gets one turn per round, even after a six.

    Local games use classic rolls. In AI games, each side gets two rerolls per match. After seeing a roll, keep it or spend a reroll. The replacement roll is final.
