Download **Salpakan.exe** for Windows 10/11 x64 or **Salpakan.apk** for Android 9 and later. Both include the game and work offline. Windows uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. Android uses an up-to-date Android System WebView or Chrome.

Use **Back** during deployment to revisit the difficulty or local mode choice. **Arrange by rank** sorts your own pieces, and **Rules & ranks** includes beginner capture examples and the full army in strength order.

On launch, choose **Player vs AI** or **Two Players**. Player vs AI opens a second screen for **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard**. Choose a level to start setting up your pieces. Two Players opens the private handoff to Ivory. Use **Back** to change modes before starting. During a game, **New match** reopens these choices and **Return to match** keeps your current board.

The Windows executable is unsigned. The Android APK is signed with an Android development certificate for direct installation. SHA256SUMS.txt lists the checksums of both tested downloads.

During setup, choose **Manual placement** for a blank formation. Pick a rank and tap a square in your three home rows. You can replace or remove pieces. When all 21 are placed, choose **Use formation**, then **Lock formation**. **Cancel placement** restores the previous setup. **Shuffle** is still available during setup.
